# Untitled Goblin Revenge Fantasy

You play as Untitled Goblin (UG). As a child her family was killed by a group of adventures lead by Main Villain (MV). Since then she's spent her whole life working to get her revenge. The game starts as you enter a pre-cognitive trance and see how the future could play out.

The main page of the game is MV's wikipedia page and you click on different links to go play out different scenes manipulating them and changing the wiki page. There is also an expected value score. At first you are trying to minimize (create as much suffering as possible for MV), and the explicit narrative will reward that kind of play, but the player will also have the option to change things to maximize positive impact instead.

Possible endings:
- Kill him as fast as possible
- Make his life as miserable as possible
- Fall in love and marry him
- Make him fall in love and break his heart
- Use your powers to help him make the world a better place
